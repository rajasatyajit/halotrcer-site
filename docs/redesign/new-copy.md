# M8: copy I wrote

Every sentence, heading and label that M8 (`m8-redesign`) adds to the three sites and that I
wrote, with the page, the exact text, and the sample field or claim it relies on. "No factual
claim" means the text states nothing about the products, the samples or the services.

## How this list was made

`main` and `m8-redesign` were each built in both modes (`npm run build`, pre-launch, and
`npm run build:launched-fixture`). The visible text, page titles, meta descriptions,
`aria-label`s and `alt` text of every page were extracted, and each sentence on the branch was
checked against all of `main`'s text. The string literals the islands and `src/lib/og.ts` add
were read from the diff. Share images show only the page title, description and stamp, which are
covered by this list.

Not listed, because I didn't write it:

- **Owner copy** from `docs/redesign/DESIGN.md` §5 and the mockup
  (`docs/redesign/reference/case-file.html`). That covers:
  - the hero heading and line (claims `www.case.title`, `www.case.lede`);
  - "Open your case", "Copy this case file", "Copy email", "Book a call";
  - "Hire the detectives", the three price kickers ("Your bill or latency", "Your customer's
    security review", "Both detectives") and "What if you stop maintaining it?";
  - the subject cards "Your AI" ("Slow, expensive or leaking? All three leave fingerprints."),
    "Your LLM endpoint" and "Your agents' tools";
  - the card kinds (Suspect #1, Suspect #2, Evidence, Accomplice, Caught, Alibi) and the titles
    "Repeated prompts", "Rush hour" and "Credential in arguments";
  - "Detectives on this case: Tokvera + Sievant." and "Who's draining yours?";
  - the continuity answer (claims `www.continuity` and `www.continuity.prelaunch`, your wording
    and your "will be" decision);
  - "Talk to us about referrals" (O1);
  - the text changed by your decisions of 2026-10-24 (see the next section).
- **Product text:**
  - the synced `SECURITY.md` pages (`/docs/security` on both product sites);
  - the tools' own finding, gap and fix text, rendered from `content/samples/*.json`.
- **Text that existed on `main`** and only moved or was restyled.

Where my text adapts owner wording, the Note column says so.

on `main`; the change to that paragraph fixed it.
