## The question

Tokvera prints a grade and Halotrcer ranks findings, and both carry a provisional label. This post will explain what the label means, why it is there, and what has to happen before it goes away.

## Planned sections

- **What is scored today:** Tokvera's pillars, checks, grade bands and mandatory-minimum caps; Halotrcer's tiers and deal-risk tags.
- **Why provisional:** the weights and thresholds have not been calibrated against real pilot scans yet, and the label says so in every report.
- **What calibration means here:** what data would be needed, and how a change to the scoring would be versioned so old reports stay readable.
- **Why there is no savings figure:** what a tool can measure on your endpoint, and what it cannot know about your traffic and contracts.
- **How to use a provisional grade:** compare runs of the same version, read the checks rather than the letter, and act on the ranked findings.
